# Color

Use OKLCH for new colour decisions where the project supports it; when
extending a system, keep its token format.

**Contrast.** Body text needs 4.5:1 against its background, placeholder text
included. Large text (24 CSS px, or about 18.7 CSS px bold) needs 3:1. Muted
grey text on a tinted near-white fails most often: measure it.

**Tinted neutrals.** A little chroma toward the brand's hue gives
backgrounds and surfaces depth. Warm or cool them only for a reason.

**Dark or light.** For a new identity, decide from who uses it, where, under
what light, and in what mood.

**Colour strategy** (for a new or replacement identity):
- **Restrained**: tinted neutrals and one accent on at most 10% of the
  surface. The product default.
- **Committed**: one saturated colour carries 30-60% of the surface. The
  brand default.
- **Full palette**: 3-4 named roles, each deliberate. Campaigns, data viz.
- **Drenched**: the surface is the colour. Brand heroes, campaign pages.
