# Read Surfaces: HTML Reports, Docs, Articles

People come to these pages to understand something and decide, and the
writing carries them.

- Open with the answer in words, not with a hero stat block or a card grid of
  numbers. A number earns emphasis when it is the answer.
- Use cards, callouts, and coloured boxes for content that is different in
  kind (a warning, a decision to make), not as a frame around every section.
- A "Key takeaways" or "Summary" box that restates the page is filler: make
  the opening carry it.
- Emoji and decorative icons beside headings add nothing a heading that
  states its point doesn't. Number sections when readers cross-reference them
  or follow a real sequence, not as decoration.
- Keep a readable column for prose; put wide tables and diagrams outside it
  rather than shrinking them into it.
