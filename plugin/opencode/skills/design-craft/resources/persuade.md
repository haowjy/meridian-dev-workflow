# Persuade Surfaces: Landing and Marketing Pages

People arrive here to decide whether the product is for them. Expression is
welcome; templates make the page read as generated.

- A hero metric and a grid of identical cards belong only where the content
  has that shape, not as default page scaffolding.
- Tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows and numbered section markers belong only
  where they orient the reader or mark a real sequence.
- A page with a story or a transformation to show can play as a film as the
  visitor scrolls (`resources/motion/cinematic-scroll.md`). That choice sets
  the page's pace and costs real build time, so it belongs to whoever owns
  the page's direction: offer it with what it gains and costs, and build a
  scroll film only when they choose it.
